Day 1

Parade

The parade is one of the highlights of AKWE, bringing students together to showcase their school spirit, creativity, and unity. Participants proudly represent their teams through colorful outfits, banners, chants, and lively performances as they march around the campus. It creates an exciting atmosphere and officially sets the energy for the celebration.

Day 1

Street Dance

The Street Dance is a lively and energetic AKWE activity where students showcase their creativity, talent, and teamwork through dance. Each team performs a fun and exciting routine while representing their group with confidence and school spirit. It brings everyone together through music, movement, and friendly competition.

Day 1

Unity Dance Field Demonstration

The Unity Dance Field Demonstration brings students together in a coordinated performance that highlights unity, teamwork, and school spirit. Participants perform synchronized movements and formations across the field, creating an energetic and colorful presentation. It is a celebration of cooperation, friendship, and the strong bond shared by the AKWE community.

Day 1

Team Building

Team Building activities bring students together through fun and challenging games that encourage cooperation, communication, and trust. Participants work as a team, support one another, and build stronger friendships while enjoying exciting activities. It promotes unity, teamwork, and camaraderie among the AKWE participants.

Day 1

Awarding

The Awarding Ceremony was a proud and memorable moment for the College of Informatics and Computing Innovations (CICI). CICI proudly secured 1st Place in the Street Dance Competition and became the Champion in the Field Demonstration. These achievements reflect the dedication, teamwork, creativity, and school spirit of the students who worked together to represent CICI with pride.
